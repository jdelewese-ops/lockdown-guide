# Identity Lockdown Checklist

A free, one-page checklist that walks people through the freezes and alerts a standard credit freeze misses. Written for older adults and the family members helping them.

**Live site:** https://jdelewese-ops.github.io/lockdown-guide/

## The 8 steps

1. Claim your my Social Security account
2. Freeze credit at Equifax, Experian and TransUnion
3. Freeze ChexSystems (bank accounts)
4. Freeze NCTUE (phone, cable and utility accounts)
5. Freeze LexisNexis
6. Get an IRS Identity Protection PIN
7. Turn on bank and card alerts
8. Keep your freeze PINs in one safe place

## Privacy

No accounts, cookies or analytics. Checkmarks are saved only in the visitor's own browser.

## Keeping it accurate

Links and phone numbers were checked on October 27, 2026. Companies change their sites, so recheck each link before sharing widely.

## Editing

Everything is in `index.html`: plain HTML, CSS and a small script. No build step.
